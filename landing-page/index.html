Core Pillars

Designed with absolute privacy in mind

Every feature is built from the ground up to protect your digital footprint. Communication should be instant, human, and ephemeral.

Account-Free Access

No phone numbers, emails, or Google sign-ins. Just install, set your preferences, and start matching. Your identity is hidden behind a random animal alias.

P2P Voice & Text Channels

Engage in crystal-clear audio calls or fast text chats. Using direct WebRTC connections, your data goes directly peer-to-peer, routing around servers to minimize latency.

Zero Logged Data

Every message, signal, and audio stream is processed solely in-memory. Once you click "Leave Chat" or close the browser window, all traces disappear from the universe instantly.

Interest Matching

Filter matches by specific interests and niche tags. Match with developers, gamers, movie buffs, or discuss specific topics without wading through generic chatrooms.

Matching Preferences

Toggle matching parameters to filter partners based on gender. Tailor your matchmaking preferences dynamically on the fly directly inside your floating panel.

Independent App Window

Opens as a standalone, borderless floating window. Drag it to your second monitor, collapse it during a call, and browse in other tabs without ever losing the conversation.
